Developer settings

The chart always runs from 5:00 to 5:00 +1d (5:00 the next morning). A clock time before 05:00 belongs to the previous service day, so 01:00 is shown as 1:00 +1d. The chart includes the full 24-hour service day; trips outside this window receive a warning. Intervals are [arrival, departure). Direct overlaps and gaps shorter than 4 minutes get red borders. Buffer overlaps with gaps of 4 minutes or more get orange borders. Third-party (TPP) services have their buffer built in, so two TPPs conflict only when they directly overlap (orange if 1 minute or less, red if longer). A TPP against a GO trip follows the normal rules above. Anything involving Reserved Coach Canada is only ever an orange warning. Deadheads / pull-ins on Platform 1 also get an orange warning in the overlap list. Parts A, B and C of one quick cut are approved against each other automatically. Layover QQ is never checked for conflicts. Pull-ins (V6.2): a Trip Stops row of Type Pull-in that leaves the terminal in the same block when a trip arrives turns that arrival into a "Pull-in … Arriving as …" visit on the pull-in platform, and it is exported the same way. Trips that run past midnight (for example 23:59 to 00:09) are drawn and exported on the +1d side of the chart. Approved conflicts stay in the overlap log in dark green and get a green border on the board. Zero-duration visits receive the stated assumption, with source assumptions retained in the details. Trips whose known arrival and departure platforms differ are placed in Unassigned until manually assigned; unknown next-departure assignments remain provisional. Trips with no arrival platform also appear in the Unassigned column. MTC allocations cover platforms 1–7. “Mute zone warnings” skips allocation confirmation pop-ups only; red restricted headers and Platform 1 deadhead warnings remain active. Departure markers are not occupancy/conflict claims. Empty operating codes mean all days; otherwise shared code characters imply simultaneous operation. For the Master TPP List, “Op Day” may hold the provider name (it sets the block colour); “Route and Variant” is not an operating code. Source zone-range checks are advisory. Rebuilding resets scenario edits and the session log.

Checkpoints · final changes and view settings

Compact saves include all final record/marker differences, linked boundaries, conflict approvals, "Entered into HASTUS" ticks, the GO/TPP mode, the active tool, selections and every view and build setting. Matching source files are required. Intermediate undo history is not exported; restored final changes remain undoable.

Drag any part of a slot sideways to move it to another column, or click a slot and use “Change platform”. Times are always locked: only the platform changes, and linked buffers follow (departure markers are not drawn on the chart). The Cut tool (C) splits a trip into linked Arrival, Layover and Departure pieces; with that tool active you can drag the top and bottom of the Layover piece, the bottom of the Arrival piece and the top of the Departure piece. Buffer lengths are fixed. Trips with no platform in the source, or with different arrival and departure platforms, sit in the Unassigned column until you assign them. Every edit can be undone. Ctrl + scroll (or pinch) zooms the chart; zooming out stops once every column / the whole day is visible.

4 · Final scenario changes

Only net changes from the imported schedule to the current scenario are listed below. Intermediate steps are omitted, and returning a trip to its starting state removes its row. Undo and redo remain available and update this comparison. Rebuilding or reloading starts a new scenario. The newest change is at the top. “-” in a To / New column means that entry did not change at that level. “Entered into HASTUS” ticks are for your own tracking only; they are kept until you rebuild or restore, and they are included in the Excel export. A tick clears itself if that trip is changed again.
